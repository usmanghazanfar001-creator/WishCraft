Birthday GIF Maker With Name and Photo

Turn a birthday poster into a looping animated GIF in your browser. Add a name, a photo and a message, pick an effect, and download a GIF that works in most chat apps.

Make a birthday GIF

How to make an animated birthday GIF

  1. Open the poster generator and upload a photo.
  2. Type the name and write a short message.
  3. Choose a template and an effect: balloons, confetti, sparkles or hearts.
  4. Press Preview Animation to watch the loop full-screen.
  5. Press Download GIF. The GIF is built in your browser, so it may take a few seconds.

About the GIF

  • Output is 480 pixels wide and loops forever, which keeps the file small enough to share.
  • The GIF shows your poster design with the animated effect; it does not include sound.
  • For a smoother, higher-quality clip, use the video export instead.

Tips for a good birthday GIF

  • Keep the message short; text on a small animation must be readable at a glance.
  • Choose high contrast: light text on a dark template, or the reverse.
  • Use one effect, not several, so the photo stays clear.
  • Test it in the chat app where you will send it; some apps convert GIFs to video.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a birthday GIF with a name?

Open the poster generator, enter the name, add a photo and message, choose an effect and press Download GIF.

Is the GIF free and without sign-up?

Yes. It is created in your browser and does not require an account.

Can I add music to the GIF?

GIFs cannot contain sound. To share music with the birthday person, create an animated birthday wish page and upload a song.

Why did WhatsApp turn my GIF into a video?

WhatsApp often converts GIFs to short looping videos. It still loops and plays automatically.
