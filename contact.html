Requesting removal of a wish

If a wish contains your photo or information and you want it removed, email us the full link (for example https://wishcrafting.vercel.app/?w=…) and we will delete it. If the wish is yours, tell us you are its creator; if you are in the photos but did not create it, tell us that instead.

Reporting a problem

For bugs, please tell us your device, browser, what you were doing and what happened. A screenshot helps.
