Two kinds of digital birthday cards

  • Animated ecard: a link that opens a gift box, cake ceremony, photo story and your message, with music if you add it. Best for a personal surprise. Learn more.
  • Card image: a 1080 × 1350 poster-style card with a photo, name and message, which you can download, post or print. Learn more.

How to make a personalized birthday card

  1. Decide whether you want an animated ecard or a card image.
  2. Add a name and photo.
  3. Write a message that sounds like you. Use our birthday wishes generator for a start.
  4. Choose a theme or template.
  5. Share the link or download the image.

What to write in a birthday card

The best card messages mention one specific thing about the person. Start with a greeting using their name, add a short thank-you or memory, then close with a wish for their coming year.
