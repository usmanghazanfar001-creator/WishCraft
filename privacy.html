Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information WishCraft ("we", "us") handles when you use wishcrafting.vercel.app, why we handle it and what choices you have.

1. Information you provide

When you create a birthday wish, you may enter a name, age, relationship, text messages, photos, an audio file and settings such as theme and unlock time. When you press Create Wish & Generate Link, this content is uploaded and stored so that the link you share can display it. Your photos and audio are stored as files and the text and settings are stored as a small data file that is retrieved using a randomly generated identifier.

The poster generator runs in your browser. Poster images, videos and GIFs are generated on your device and are not uploaded unless you use another tool to share them.

2. Who can see your content

Anyone who has your wish link can open it and see the photos, message and audio. Only share the link with people you trust, and do not upload photos or information you would not want a recipient to forward. We do not publish a directory of wishes and we ask search engines not to index shared wishes.

3. Information stored on your device

The creator saves a draft of your work in your browser's local storage so you can come back to it. It stays on your device and can be cleared at any time with the "Start fresh" button or by clearing your browser data.

4. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider, Vercel, and our storage provider process technical information such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and timestamps to deliver the site, keep it secure and diagnose errors.

5. Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show advertising on our guides and information pages. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland may be shown a consent message so they can choose whether to allow advertising cookies. We do not display ads inside the wish creator or on shared wish pages.

6. Third-party services

The site uses services including Vercel (hosting and file storage), Google AdSense (advertising) and content delivery networks that supply open-source code libraries used for image and GIF export. These services have their own privacy policies.

7. How we use information

  • To create, store and display the wishes you make
  • To operate, secure and improve the site
  • To respond to messages you send us
  • To show advertising that supports the free service

We do not sell personal information and we do not use your photos or messages for advertising.

8. Retention and removal

Wishes remain available so that links keep working. You can ask us to delete a wish at any time by emailing us the link; see our contact page. We may also remove content that is reported or that violates our terms.

9. Children

WishCraft is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you share photos of a child, make sure you are their parent or guardian or have the guardian's permission. If you believe a child's information has been uploaded without permission, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at usmanghazanfar001@gmail.com.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email usmanghazanfar001@gmail.com.
