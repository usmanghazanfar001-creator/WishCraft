Why personal wishes matter

A text that says "Happy birthday" takes two seconds to send and two seconds to forget. A wish with a photo from a trip you took together, a song they love and a message that mentions something only you two know takes a few minutes to make, and people tend to remember it, replay it and show it to others.

WishCraft is built to make that effort small. You don't need design skills or editing software, and the person receiving it doesn't need an account or an app. The animations, cake ceremony and photo story give your words a moment to land, rather than burying them in a chat thread.

Common questions

What is WishCraft?

WishCraft is a free web tool for creating animated birthday wishes and birthday posters. You add a name, photos, a message and optional music, preview the result, and share it with a single link. You can also make a poster image, a short video or a GIF.

Is WishCraft really free?

Yes. You can create wishes and posters without paying and without creating an account. The site shows advertising on its guide and information pages to cover running costs.

Do I need to sign up or install anything?

No. WishCraft runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. Recipients just open the link.

What do I need to make a wish?

Only the birthday person's name is required. Photos, a song, a message, themes, animations and bonus scenes are all optional.

How many photos can I add?

One main photo and up to ten memory photos. Photos are resized and compressed automatically so the wish loads quickly on mobile data.
