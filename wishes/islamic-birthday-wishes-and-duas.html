Islamic Birthday Wishes & Duas: 25 Blessings for Muslim Friends & Family

By WishCraft · Updated October 31, 2026 · Tap and hold any message to copy it

Many Muslims send birthday wishes in the form of duas: prayers for health, guidance and blessing. A good Islamic birthday message focuses on gratitude to Allah, wishes for a long and healthy life and hopes for a year of barakah (blessing).

A respectful note: scholars and families hold different views on marking birthdays. Some celebrate with family, some simply offer duas, and some prefer not to mark the day at all. If you're not sure how the person feels, a quiet dua is almost always welcome.

Islamic birthday wishes for a friend

  • May Allah bless you with health, happiness and guidance in the year ahead. Happy birthday!
  • May Allah fill your life with barakah and keep you close to Him. Happy birthday, my friend.
  • Wishing you a year of peace, good health and Allah's mercy. Happy birthday!
  • May Allah grant all your halal wishes and keep you safe from harm. Happy birthday!
  • I'm grateful to Allah for your friendship. May He bless you always.
  • May Allah make this year your best, in this life and the next. Happy birthday!

Duas for parents on their birthday

  • May Allah grant you a long life filled with health, ease and happiness. Happy birthday, Ammi.
  • May Allah shower you with His mercy and reward you for all you've given us. Happy birthday, Abu.
  • O Allah, bless my parents and keep them safe. May this year bring them peace and joy.
  • May Allah give you strength and keep your smile. Happy birthday, Ammi jaan.

Islamic birthday wishes for a spouse

  • May Allah keep us together in happiness and faith. Happy birthday, my love.
  • May Allah bless you with health, success and contentment. Happy birthday, my dear husband.
  • May Allah fill your life with joy and ease. Happy birthday, my dear wife.

Islamic birthday wishes for children

  • May Allah make you righteous, healthy and a source of comfort to others. Happy birthday, dear child.
  • May Allah protect you and guide you always. Happy birthday, my little one.
  • May Allah grant you beneficial knowledge and a kind heart. Happy birthday!

Arabic phrases you can include

  • بارك الله في عمركBarak Allahu fi umrik: May Allah bless your life.
  • كل عام وأنتم بخيرKullu aam wa antum bi-khair: May you be well every year.
  • جزاك الله خيراًJazak Allahu khayran: May Allah reward you with good.
  • تقبل الله منا ومنكمTaqabbal Allahu minna wa minkum: May Allah accept from us and you.

Short Islamic birthday messages

  • May Allah bless your year. Happy birthday!
  • Wishing you health, peace and barakah.
  • May Allah keep you happy always.

Tips to make your message more personal

  • Keep it sincere and simple; a short dua often means more than a long message.
  • Be mindful of the person's views on celebrating birthdays.
  • Avoid sending music or party-themed content if you know it won't be appreciated.
  • Add the person's name; it makes a dua personal.
