Short Birthday Wishes & Captions for Instagram, WhatsApp and Cards

By WishCraft · Updated October 31, 2026 · Tap and hold any message to copy it

Sometimes short is perfect: a caption under a photo, a line on a card, a WhatsApp status or a quick text. A good short wish is simple, warm and specific enough that it doesn't feel mass-produced.

Here are 45 options sorted by use: general, for Instagram captions, for WhatsApp status and for cards.

Tips to make your message more personal

  • Add the person's name to make any short line personal.
  • Match the platform: playful for Instagram, simple for WhatsApp, warm for cards.
  • Add an emoji or two only if that's how you normally write.
  • If you can, pair your caption with a real photo of the two of you.
