Funny Birthday Wishes: 35 Hilarious Messages That Won't Offend

By WishCraft · Updated October 31, 2026 · Tap and hold any message to copy it

Funny birthday wishes work when the joke is on the situation or on you, not on the person. Age, cake, candles and the passage of time are fair game; weight, looks and sensitive topics aren't. If you're not sure how they'll react, pick the gentler lines.

Here are 35 jokes organized by who they're for, from friends who love a roast to colleagues who need a gentle laugh.

Funny birthday wishes for friends

  • Happy birthday! You're not old, you're pre-owned and well maintained.
  • I'd say you're getting older, but you've been acting 70 for years. Happy birthday!
  • Another year, another candle I'm worried about setting off the fire alarm with. Happy birthday!
  • Age is just a number. A really big, slightly alarming number. Happy birthday!
  • Happy birthday! Let's get cake and complain about how nothing hurt when we were younger.
  • I'd get you a gift, but you already have everything. Except my money. Happy birthday!
  • You've reached the age where you can't remember where you left your phone, but it's in your hand. Happy birthday!
  • Happy birthday! May your wine be older than you and your cheese be stronger than your knees.

Funny birthday wishes for family

  • Happy birthday! You're proof that some things really do get better with age. Or at least louder.
  • Another year of pretending you understand the TV remote. Happy birthday!
  • Happy birthday! I'm proud to say I knew you before you needed reading glasses.
  • You're not getting older, you're just becoming more qualified to give advice. Happy birthday!
  • They say you're only as old as you feel. In that case, I'm so sorry. Happy birthday!
  • Happy birthday! May your cake have fewer candles than a bonfire. But the same level of excitement.

Funny birthday wishes for coworkers

Safe, light and office-friendly.

  • Happy birthday! May your day be free of meetings and full of cake.
  • Hope you get as much cake as you have unread emails.
  • Happy birthday! Today, you have permission to leave early. I'm not your manager, but I support you.
  • Wishing you a birthday as smooth as a project that finished on time.
  • Happy birthday! Please accept this message as a gift. It's non-refundable.

Funny birthday wishes for a partner

  • Happy birthday! Thanks for being my favorite person to argue about pizza toppings with.
  • You're still the one I want to steal blankets from. Happy birthday!
  • Happy birthday to the only person who can make me laugh and roll my eyes at the same time.
  • Another year older, but still the one I'd choose to be stuck in traffic with. Happy birthday!

Funny birthday captions

  • Don't count the candles, enjoy the light.
  • I'm not old, I'm a classic.
  • Calories don't count on birthdays. Science.
  • Cake first, adulting later.
  • Born to be fabulous. Aged to perfection.
  • Level up! New age unlocked.
  • Making wishes and eating cake. Nothing else is required.

Tips to make your message more personal

  • Aim the joke at the situation or at yourself, not at their looks.
  • Know your audience. A joke for a close friend can be too much for a boss.
  • Pair a joke with a sincere closing line: "…but seriously, I'm glad you're in my life."
  • If you're in doubt, don't send the joke.
