Birthday Wishes for a Son or Daughter: 28 Messages From Mom & Dad

By WishCraft · Updated October 31, 2026 · Tap and hold any message to copy it

Writing to your own child is one of the sweetest and hardest birthday messages. You want to tell them how proud you are without making it heavy. The best ones are warm, specific and forward-looking: what you love about who they are, and what you hope for the year ahead.

Below are messages for sons, daughters, young children and grown-up kids.

Birthday wishes for a son

  • Happy birthday to my son. You've made me prouder every year. Never stop being curious and kind.
  • Watching you grow is the greatest joy of my life. Happy birthday, my boy.
  • You are brave, thoughtful and strong. I can't wait to see what you do this year. Happy birthday!
  • Whatever you do, know I'm always on your side. Happy birthday, son.
  • The day you were born, my life changed forever for the better. Happy birthday!
  • I hope this year brings you great friends, big adventures and plenty of cake.

Birthday wishes for a daughter

  • Happy birthday to my beautiful daughter. You've filled my life with joy and light.
  • You are smart, kind and capable of anything. I'm so proud to be your parent. Happy birthday!
  • My heart grew the day you were born. Happy birthday, my princess.
  • Never doubt how loved you are. Happy birthday, my darling.
  • I'm excited to watch your dreams come true this year. Happy birthday!
  • You make everyone around you smile. Happy birthday to my sunshine.

For a young child

  • Happy birthday, little star! May your day be full of games, giggles and cake.
  • Another year of adventures begins today. Happy birthday, champ!
  • You're our favorite person in the whole world. Happy birthday!
  • Let's make today as magical as you are. Happy birthday!

For a grown-up son or daughter

  • I look at the person you've become and I'm amazed. Happy birthday. I'm proud of you every day.
  • You'll always be my baby, even when you're taller than me. Happy birthday!
  • Thank you for the person you are and the joy you bring. Happy birthday!
  • May this year bring you success, peace and everything you work for.

Prayers for a child

  • May Allah protect you, guide you and bless you with success. Happy birthday, my dear child.
  • My duas are with you today and always. Happy birthday, jaan.

Short messages and captions

  • Happy birthday to my greatest gift!
  • My heart, my pride, my joy. Happy birthday!
  • Proud of you always. Happy birthday!

Tips to make your message more personal

  • Tell your child something specific you admire about them.
  • For young children, keep it short and playful.
  • For adult children, tell them you're proud of the person, not just their achievements.
  • Add a photo from when they were small.
