How the generator works

The generator picks from our hand-written birthday wishes for each relationship and style, then adds the name you enter. It is not AI-written, and that is deliberate: every line has been written and checked by a person. Press the button again for a new set.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an AI birthday wishes generator?

No. It assembles messages from our library of hand-written birthday wishes and inserts the name you type. That keeps the writing natural and consistent. You can edit any result before using it.

Can I generate birthday wishes with a name?

Yes. Type the name, choose who it is for and the style, and the generator personalizes each message.

Can I use the messages on a poster or birthday link?

Yes. Copy a message and paste it into the WishCraft wish creator or poster generator.

Are the messages unique?

They are written by us and drawn from our wish collections. Add a memory or detail to make yours one of a kind.
