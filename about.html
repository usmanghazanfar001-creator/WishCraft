About WishCraft

WishCraft started with a simple observation: most birthday messages are forgettable not because people don't care, but because the tools for sending something more personal are either complicated or expensive. We wanted to make it easy to turn a few photos, a song and a few honest sentences into a small moment someone will remember.

What we make

  • The wish creator: an animated, shareable birthday experience with a gift box, a cake ceremony, a photo memory story and a personal message.
  • The poster generator: a quick way to design a birthday poster and export it as an image, short video or GIF.
  • The guides: free articles on writing messages, choosing photos, designing posters and planning surprises.

What we believe

  • Personal beats polished. A sincere line with a real memory is better than a perfect template.
  • No barriers. No sign-up, no app to install, nothing for the recipient to download.
  • Respect for privacy. We only store what is needed to make your link work, and we explain it plainly in our privacy policy.
  • Honest content. Our guides are written to help, not to stuff keywords.

How we make money

WishCraft is free to use. We show advertising on our guides and information pages. The wish creator and recipient experience are kept free of ads so the moment stays about the person being celebrated.

Who we are

WishCraft is an independent project that loves celebrations and simple, well-made web tools. Feedback from the people who use it decides what gets built next.
