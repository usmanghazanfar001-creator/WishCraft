How to choose a template

  • Parents and elders: Elegant Gold, Royal Gold or Midnight Luxury feel classic and respectful.
  • Partners: Rose Romance, Romantic Pink or Rose Gold feel warm and personal.
  • Friends: Colorful, Neon Party or Tropical Sunrise add energy.
  • Colleagues: Minimal White or Cloud White keep things clean and professional.

Contrast matters more than color: pick a template where text is easy to read. For layout advice read our poster design guide.

Frequently asked questions

How many birthday templates does WishCraft have?

Nine poster templates and 22 themes for animated birthday wishes, plus frame styles, photo shapes, fonts and animation effects you can combine.

Are the templates free?

Yes. All templates and themes are free to use, with no account required.

Can I change the colors or fonts?

You can choose between templates, three font styles, text alignment and photo shapes. Colors are set by the template.

Do the previews show real birthdays?

No. The previews use a sample name and placeholder cake image so you can see each style. Your photo and text replace them.
