Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers about how WishCraft works. If you can't find yours, get in touch.

What is WishCraft?

WishCraft is a free web tool for creating animated birthday wishes and birthday posters. You add a name, photos, a message and optional music, preview the result, and share it with a single link. You can also make a poster image, a short video or a GIF.

Is WishCraft really free?

Yes. You can create wishes and posters without paying and without creating an account. The site shows advertising on its guide and information pages to cover running costs.

Do I need to sign up or install anything?

No. WishCraft runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. Recipients just open the link.

What do I need to make a wish?

Only the birthday person's name is required. Photos, a song, a message, themes, animations and bonus scenes are all optional.

How many photos can I add?

One main photo and up to ten memory photos. Photos are resized and compressed automatically so the wish loads quickly on mobile data.

Can I add my own music?

Yes. You can upload an audio file up to 25 MB and choose the start and end points. If you don't add a song you can turn on a built-in music-box version of Happy Birthday. Only upload music you have the right to share.

Why doesn't the music start automatically for the recipient?

Most phones and browsers block sound until the person taps the screen. WishCraft starts with an Open button so the music can begin right after the first tap.

How long does a shared link last?

Wishes are stored so the link keeps working. We may remove old or reported content. If you want your wish removed, contact us with the link and we will delete it.

Who can see my wish?

Anyone who has the link can open it, so share it only with the people you intend. Links are randomly generated and are not listed anywhere, and shared wishes are asked not to be indexed by search engines.

Can I schedule a wish for the birthday itself?

Yes. Use the lock option to choose a date and time. If someone opens the link early they will see a countdown instead of the surprise.

Does the poster generator work on a phone?

Yes. You can design the poster on your phone and download it as a 1080 × 1350 image. Video and GIF export run in your browser and may take a few seconds, depending on your device.

I lost my half-finished wish. Can I get it back?

The creator auto-saves a draft on the device and browser you were using. Open the creator again on the same browser and use the draft bar at the top to continue.

What languages are supported?

The interface is in English, and your message can be in any language your device can type. There is also an Urdu quick-message style to help you get started.

How do I report a problem or request removal?

Visit the contact page and include the link. We review requests as quickly as we can.
