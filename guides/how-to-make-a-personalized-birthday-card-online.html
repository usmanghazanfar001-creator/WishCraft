How to Make a Personalized Birthday Card Online (Free, in 5 Steps)

By WishCraft · Updated October 23, 2026

A personalized birthday card says more than a store-bought one because it has their name, your photo together and words that sound like you. You can make one online in about ten minutes. This guide covers two kinds: an animated ecard sent as a link, and a poster-style card image you can post or print.

Choose your card type

Card typeWhat you getBest for
Animated ecard (link)Gift box, cake ceremony, photo story, music and message in one private linkA real surprise; far-away friends and family
Poster-style card (image)1080 × 1350 image with photo, name and message; also video and GIFSocial posts, WhatsApp status, printing small copies

How to make a personalized birthday card

  1. Pick the card type. For the ecard, open the wish creator. For an image, switch to the Poster Generator tab or use the poster maker.
  2. Add the name and a photo. Use a clear, well-lit portrait. Our design guide covers cropping and layout.
  3. Write the message. Keep it to a few sentences. Need ideas? Try the birthday wishes generator or browse wishes by person.
  4. Choose the look. Pick a template or theme that fits the person: classic gold for parents, bright colors for friends, soft pastels for a sister.
  5. Share or download. Send the ecard link on WhatsApp, or download the poster image.

What makes a card feel personal

  • Use their nickname if you have one.
  • Mention a memory in one sentence.
  • Pick a photo with a story. A candid shot beats a posed one.
  • Sign it yourself. "From Ali" is warmer than "From your friends" when it is just you.

Examples of what to write

Happy birthday, Hira! Thank you for driving two hours to help me move last spring. I hope this year is as generous to you as you are to everyone else.
Happy birthday, Dad. You taught me how to fix a bike and, without noticing, how to fix most other things. I hope this year gives you rest.

More examples: friend, father, sister.

Printing your card

The poster image is 1080 × 1350 pixels, which prints clearly at small sizes such as A6 or 4 × 5 inches. For larger prints, use a design program that can export at higher resolution.

Privacy tips

An ecard link can be opened by anyone who has it, so send it directly to the person. Don't post it in public groups if it has private photos. See our privacy policy.
