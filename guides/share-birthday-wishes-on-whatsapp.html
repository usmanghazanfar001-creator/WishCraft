How to Share Birthday Wishes on WhatsApp (Timing, Etiquette & Privacy)

By WishCraft · Updated October 23, 2026

WhatsApp is where most birthday wishes are sent today. A few small decisions about timing, format and privacy can turn a quick message into one that feels considered. This guide covers all three.

When to send

  • First thing in the morning is the classic choice. It shows you remembered and gives your message a chance to be among the first they read.
  • Midnight suits close friends and partners. Only do it if you know they won't be asleep with notifications blinking, or if you don't mind them seeing it in the morning.
  • Evening works for people with busy days. A thoughtful late message is better than a rushed early one.
  • Across time zones, send it when it's morning where they are, not where you are.

Choose the right format

FormatBest for
Text messageQuick, personal wishes to friends and relatives
Voice noteParents, grandparents and anyone who loves hearing your voice

Sharing a WishCraft link

After you generate your link in the creator, use the WhatsApp button to open WhatsApp with a ready message and your link, or Copy Message to paste it yourself. WhatsApp will show a link preview. Add a short line before the link so the recipient knows what to expect, for example: "I made something for your birthday. Open it with the sound on."

The recipient taps the link and starts the experience on their phone. Browsers often block music until the first tap, so the wish begins with an "Open" button.

Sharing a poster

Download the poster as an image and send it as a photo, not a document, so it displays in full. For a status update, upload the image or the short looping video. If you send a GIF, WhatsApp may convert it to a short video; that's normal.

Group chat etiquette

  • Check whether the group has a collective message planned before sending your own.
  • Avoid sharing embarrassing photos or private jokes in a large group.
  • Don't tag people in photos they haven't agreed to share.
  • If a surprise is planned, don't write about it in a chat that includes the birthday person.

Keeping photos and links private

Anyone who has a link can open it, so treat it like a private photo album:

  • Send the link directly to the recipient rather than posting it in a public group or status.
  • Avoid including photos of children or sensitive moments unless the parents or participants have agreed.
  • Remember that recipients can forward the link. Choose content you'd be comfortable seeing shared.

Follow up

After the birthday, a short "Did you like it?" message is a nice touch. In WishCraft the recipient can tap a "Say Thanks" button at the end of the experience, which opens a pre-written thank-you message to you.

Quick checklist before you press send

  1. Is the name spelled correctly?
  2. Is it the right time of day for them?
  3. Did you test the link on your phone?
  4. Is the message personal, not copied?
  5. Are you sending it to the right chat?
